Logo Design

I was asked to create a logo for a local photographer who was in need of a logo. This logo would serve a watermark for her images. It is meant to stop image theft, build brand awareness, and ensure proper credit when photos appear online.

Early Concepts

Melina prefered fluid logos that interlinked both of her initials. She wanted something clever and also would reflect her photography style. She wants to photography sports, weddings and graduates so it was important that the logo was not too blocky or florid so that it could be applied to a range of photos.
