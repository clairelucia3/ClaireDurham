Upper Dublin Chinese-American Association

This redesign for the Upper Dublin Chinese American Association focused on creating a visual identity that honors cultural heritage while introducing a modern presence. Drawing inspiration from traditional Chinese seal stamps and geometric shapes, the logo system balances structure, and symbolism. This rebrand included various logos as well as colors, typography, and patterns.
