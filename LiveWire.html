LiveWire Social Media

LiveWire is a wire harness software design tool that is used by engineers to save time while building cables or wire harnesses. They asked for a variety of social media templates to be posted on their LinkedIn. I created styled image frames, seamless carousels, and posts that were centered around copy or images. Templates were made in Adobe Illustrator and included multiple variations of color.

Constraints

The brand uses some very vibrant colors and it was important that the right shades were being used. This allows the colors to keep their eye-catching ability without losing their legibility. I also had to consider if the post was image based or text focused.

Solutions

I made sure to that the neon green was being used for both light and dark backgrounds. I also made sure that there many options of posts that varied in text length, image size, singular slides and carousels.

White Paper Template

For Mama Mir’s, I brought the brand to life on social media by creating fun, eye-catching content and advertisements that made people crave more than just food. The ads made them want to be part of the community. From graphics to videos, I highlighted everything from the handmade meals to the catered dishes. My goal was to create a cohesive brand image and social media presence that shows how Mama Mir's convienant meals can bring people together.

I designed a variety of targeted social media ads for Mama Mir’s Kitchen, from promoting space rentals and back-to-school meal prep for busy families to showcasing catering for sports teams and special events.

Constraints

One challenge with White Papers is that it is extremely text heavy and hierarchy is very important.

Solutions

To ensure that the text guided the viewer’s eye from the main title down to supporting details, I varied font sizes, bold colors/highlights and applied generous amounts of negative space.
