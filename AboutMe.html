Hello,

My name is Claire Durham, a Graphic Designer with a passion for telling stories with their work. I recently graduated from La Salle University in Philadelphia, where I majored in Digital Arts/Multimedia and Art History. I was also a member of the Division 1 Water Polo team and Honors Program. I graduated Maxima Cum Laude and was awarded the Outstanding Graduate of the Art History Program. Since graduation, I have had the priveledge to work at Re:Build Tekna where I am continuing to grow and learn as a Graphic Designer and professional.

My journey in design began with a passion for fine arts. I started painting in highschool and knew that I was meant to have a creative career. As I began my college search, I was recruited to La Salle University and began studying Art History. I loved studying the arts but found that I spent much of class time, just waiting to go home and create and design myself. So after my first semester, I added a major in Digital Arts and Multimedia. Since then, I have grown a love for successful branding, textures, bold fonts, and interactive web design.

Let's work Together!
