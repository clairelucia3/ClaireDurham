I’m a graphic designer who wants to tell a story. I have a love for all things creative but a special passion for successful branding, textures, bold fonts, and interactive web design. Much of my creative career was centered around painting and I approach Graphic Design in a similiar way.

I recently graduated from La Salle University, studying both Art History and Digital Arts/Multimedia with an Honors distinction. Blending these two fields of tradition and innovation has given me a unique perspective on how art has shaped culture through history and how design continues that story today.
